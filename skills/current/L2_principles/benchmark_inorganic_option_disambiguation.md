# Benchmark Inorganic Option Disambiguation

Use this note for inorganic benchmark MCQs where the hard part is binding a
derived species or balanced equation to one statement option. Do not select a
statement from broad periodic intuition before checking the stated stoichiometry,
oxidation states, and formula constraints.

## Iodine Oxides from Ozone

For iodine/ozone rows with a three-fold-axis oxide, the mixed-valent oxide
`I(IO3)3` (`I4O9`) is a viable assignment for `A`; its iodine sites are not all
equivalent and include +3 and +5 iodine. The common high-valent follow-on oxide
`B` is `I2O5`.

When the row says `A` releases 0.75 equivalents of oxygen to form `B`, include
elemental iodine if required by atom balance:

```text
4 I4O9 -> 6 I2O5 + 3 O2 + 2 I2
```

The product-side coefficient sum is therefore 11 for the coprime integer
equation. Product statements about a higher oxidation state in `A`, identical
iodine environments in `A`, planar-triangular iodine in `B`, or polyiodine
cations in the strongly oxidizing fluorosulfate product should be checked
against this assignment rather than chosen by surface wording.
