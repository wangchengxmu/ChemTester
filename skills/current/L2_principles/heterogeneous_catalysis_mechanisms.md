## [Source: Wikipedia, Heterogeneous Catalysis]
### Key Concepts from Wikipedia
- **Sabatier principle**: Best catalyst binds intermediates with intermediate strength — not too weak (no reaction) nor too strong (products cannot desorb).
- **Volcano plots**: Activity vs. adsorption energy shows maximum at optimal binding.
- **Langmuir-Hinshelwood mechanism**: Both reactants adsorbed on surface before reaction.
- **Eley-Rideal mechanism**: One reactant adsorbed, other reacts from gas phase.
- **Mars-van Krevelen mechanism**: Reactant removes lattice atom from catalyst; lattice regenerated by second reactant.
- **Structure sensitivity**: Some reactions depend on specific crystal facets (e.g., NH₃ synthesis on Fe(111) > Fe(100)).

## L3 Tool Call Directives

**Source:** heterogeneous_catalysis_tools.py
Langmuir isotherm, BET surface area, Langmuir-Hinshelwood/Eley-Rideal rate laws, Scherrer equation, catalyst dispersion.

### Available functions:
- langmuir_coverage(pressure, k_ads) → dict — θ = KP/(1+KP)
- langmuir_dissociative_coverage(pressure, k_ads) → dict — θ = √(KP)/(1+√(KP)) for H₂ dissociation
- lh_rate(p_a, p_b, k_a, k_b, k_rate) → dict — Langmuir-Hinshelwood: r = k·Ka·Pa·Kb·Pb/(1+KaPa+KbPb)²
- er_rate(p_a, p_b, k_a, k_rate) → dict — Eley-Rideal: r = k·Ka·Pa·Pb/(1+KaPa)
- et_surface_area(v_m, c, cross_section) → dict — A = V_m·N_A·σ/V_molar (m²/g); σ(N₂)=0.162 nm²
- scherrer_crystallite_size(wavelength, fwhm, theta, k) → dict — D = Kλ/(β cosθ) (nm); K≈0.9
- catalyst_dispersion(particle_diameter_nm, density_g_cm3, molar_mass) → dict — Fraction of surface atoms

### Common errors:
- ❌ Confusing LH (both adsorbed, denominator²) with ER (one gas-phase, denominator linear)
- ❌ Scherrer angles must be in radians (not degrees) for FWHM and 2θ
