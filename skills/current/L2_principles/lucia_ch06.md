# Reaction Types, Design, and Efficiency

## Source
[Key Elements of Green Chemistry (Lucia), Chapter 6](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Environmental_Chemistry/Key_Elements_of_Green_Chemistry_(Lucia)/06%3A_Reaction_Types_Design_and_Efficiency)
Author: Lucian Lucia, North Carolina State University

## Key Concepts

### Green Reaction Design
- Maximize yield AND specificity
- Mimic biological reactions: efficient, low-energy, atom-conserving
- Consider: selectivity, energy input, waste generation, safety

### Key Green Reaction Types
1. **Click chemistry**: modular, high-yielding, stereospecific (CuAAC azide-alkyne cycloaddition)
2. **Multicomponent reactions (MCRs)**: 3+ reactants in one pot, high atom economy (Ugi, Passerini, Biginelli)
3. **Catalytic asymmetric synthesis**: enantioselective catalysts avoid resolving agents
4. **C-H activation**: functionalize directly without pre-functionalization
5. **Photocatalysis**: use visible light as energy source
6. **Electrochemistry**: electrons as reagents (no chemical oxidant/reductant waste)

### Reaction Efficiency Metrics
- **Yield**: (actual product / theoretical product) × 100%
- **Selectivity**: (desired product / all products) × 100%
- **Atom economy**: (MW product / Σ MW reactants) × 100%
- **Turnover number (TON)**: moles product / moles catalyst
- **Turnover frequency (TOF)**: TON / time
- **E-factor**: waste per unit product

### Process Intensification
- One-pot reactions (telescoping/cascade reactions)
- Continuous flow chemistry (vs. batch)
- Microwave, ultrasound, mechanochemical activation
- Combined reaction-separation processes

### Computational Reaction Design
- DFT calculations to predict reaction pathways
- Machine learning for reaction outcome prediction
- Automated synthesis planning (retrosynthesis algorithms)

## Formulas
- TON = n_product / n_catalyst
- TOF = TON / t (s⁻¹ or h⁻¹)
- Atom Economy = (MW_product / Σ MW_reactants) × 100%
- E-factor = (m_waste) / (m_product)
