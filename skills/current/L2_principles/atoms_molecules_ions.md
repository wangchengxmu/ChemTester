## Atomic Theory

1. All matter composed of atoms (Dalton)
2. Atoms of given element identical; atoms of different elements differ
3. Compounds form from specific whole-number ratios of atoms
4. Chemical reactions rearrange atoms; atoms are neither created nor destroyed

## Subatomic Particles

| Particle | Charge | Mass (amu) | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proton | +1 | ~1.0073 | Nucleus |
| Neutron | 0 | ~1.0087 | Nucleus |
| Electron | -1 | ~0.00055 | Outside nucleus |

Atomic number (Z) = number of protons. Mass number (A) = protons + neutrons.
Notation: ᴬₓX where X = element symbol.

## Isotopes and Average Atomic Mass

Isotopes: same Z, different A (different neutron count).
Average atomic mass = Σ (isotope mass × fractional abundance).

## The Mole Concept

1 mole = 6.02214076 × 10²³ entities (Avogadro's number, Nₐ).
Molar mass (M) = mass of 1 mole of a substance (g/mol).
Numerically equal to atomic/molecular/formula mass in amu.

## Molecular vs Ionic Compounds

- **Molecular:** formed between nonmetals, discrete molecules (H₂O, CO₂)
- **Ionic:** formed between metal + nonmetal, crystal lattice (NaCl, CaF₂)
- **Empirical formula:** simplest whole-number ratio of atoms
- **Molecular formula:** actual number of atoms per molecule (integer multiple of empirical)

## Chemical Nomenclature

### Ionic compounds
- Monatomic cations: element name (Na⁺ = sodium)
- Transition metals: element name(Stock number) — Fe²⁺ = iron(II)
- Monatomic anions: element stem + -ide (Cl⁻ = chloride)
- Polyatomic ions: use name from L4_reference/common_ions.csv

### Molecular compounds (nonmetal + nonmetal)
- Use Greek prefixes: mono-, di-, tri-, tetra-, penta-, hexa-, hepta-, octa-, nona-, deca-
- Second element gets -ide suffix: CO = carbon monoxide, P₄O₁₀ = tetraphosphorus decoxide

### Acids
- Binary (HX): hydro- + stem + -ic acid (HCl = hydrochloric acid)
- Oxoacids: -ic for most O, -ous for one fewer O (H₂SO₄ = sulfuric, H₂SO₃ = sulfurous)
