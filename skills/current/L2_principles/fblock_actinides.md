## Definition

The **actinide series** comprises the 14 elements from Thorium (Th, Z=90) to Lawrencium (Lr, Z=103), following Actinium (Ac, Z=89). These elements are characterized by progressive filling of the 5f orbitals and are collectively known as the **5f elements**. All actinides are radioactive.

### 3. Radioactivity and Nuclear Properties

**All actinides are radioactive:**
- Elements up to uranium (Z=92) occur naturally
- Transuranium elements (Np, Pu, etc.) are synthetic
- Nuclear instability due to large atomic number (Z > 83)

## Comparison: Lanthanides vs Actinides

| Property | Lanthanides | Actinides |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| f orbital | 4f | 5f |
| Oxidation states | +3 dominant | +3 to +7 |
| Radioactivity | None (stable) | All radioactive |
| Bonding | Primarily ionic | More covalent |
| f orbital exposure | Buried | More exposed |
| Coordination | High CN, ionic | High CN, more covalent |
| Magnetic behavior | Well-predicted | More complex |
