# Risk Assessment and Regulation

## Source
[Environmental Toxicology (van Gestel et al.), Chapter 6](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Environmental_Chemistry/Environmental_Toxicology_(van_Gestel_et_al.)/06%3A_Risk_Assessment_and_Regulation)
Authors: Sylvia Moes, Kees van Gestel, & Gerco van Beek

## Key Concepts

### Environmental Risk Assessment Framework
1. **Hazard Identification**: Does the chemical cause adverse effects?
2. **Dose-Response Assessment**: What is the relationship between dose and effect?
3. **Exposure Assessment**: How much, how often, how long are organisms exposed?
4. **Risk Characterization**: Integrate hazard and exposure to estimate risk

### Key Regulatory Concepts
- **PNEC** (Predicted No-Effect Concentration): safe concentration for the environment
- **PEC** (Predicted Environmental Concentration): expected environmental level
- **Risk Quotient**: RQ = PEC / PNEC; RQ < 1 → acceptable risk
- **Margin of Safety**: MOS = NOAEL / estimated exposure

### International Regulatory Frameworks
- **EU REACH**: Registration, Evaluation, Authorization, Restriction of Chemicals
- **US EPA**: TSCA (Toxic Substances Control Act), FIFRA (pesticides), CWA (Clean Water Act)
- **Stockholm Convention**: global treaty on persistent organic pollutants (POPs)
- **Minamata Convention**: global treaty on mercury
- **Basel Convention**: transboundary movement of hazardous wastes

### Risk Management
- Source reduction, pollution prevention
- Best available techniques (BAT) and best environmental practices (BEP)
- Remediation: soil washing, bioremediation, phytoremediation
- Monitoring and adaptive management

### Emerging Challenges
- Chemical cocktails (mixture toxicity)
- Endocrine disruptors
- Nanomaterials regulation
- Climate change interactions with chemical toxicity

## Formulas
- Risk Quotient: RQ = PEC / PNEC
- PNEC = NOAEL / AF (assessment factor: 10-1000 depending on data quality)
- Hazard Index (mixtures): HI = Σ(E_i / RfD_i)
