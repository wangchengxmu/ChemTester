## Key Concepts

Modern medicinal chemistry integrates all physical science disciplines. Human Genome Project (2003) launched multi-omics expansion. New technologies: covalent inhibitors (beyond classical occupancy), heterobifunctional ligands (PROTACs - simultaneous binding of target + modifying enzyme), proximity-induction for post-translational modifications. Fields driving innovation: proteomics, metabolomics, computational biology, AI/ML. Goal: reduce clinical attrition rates and achieve patient-specific therapy.
