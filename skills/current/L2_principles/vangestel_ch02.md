# Environmental Chemistry — Chemicals

## Source
[Environmental Toxicology (van Gestel et al.), Chapter 2](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Environmental_Chemistry/Environmental_Toxicology_(van_Gestel_et_al.)/02%3A_Environmental_Chemistry_Chemicals)
Authors: Sylvia Moes, Kees van Gestel, & Gerco van Beek

## Key Concepts

### Environmental Chemistry Fundamentals
- Study of chemical processes occurring in the environment
- Chemical speciation: different forms of an element (e.g., Cr³⁺ vs Cr⁶⁺) have vastly different toxicities
- Environmental compartments: air, water, soil, sediment, biota

### Physicochemical Properties Governing Environmental Behavior
- **Water solubility**: determines aquatic availability; inversely related to hydrophobicity
- **Vapor pressure**: controls volatilization from soil/water to atmosphere
- **Octanol-water partition coefficient (K_ow)**: key predictor of bioaccumulation and sorption
- **pKa**: determines ionization state and hence mobility in water/soil
- **Henry's Law constant (H)**: controls air-water partitioning

### Classes of Environmental Chemicals
- **Metals**: essential (Cu, Zn, Fe) vs. toxic (Pb, Hg, Cd); speciation critical
- **Organic contaminants**: PCBs, PAHs, dioxins, pesticides, pharmaceuticals
- **Inorganic ions**: nitrate, phosphate, sulfate, cyanide
- **Nanomaterials**: unique behavior due to high surface area-to-volume ratio

### Chemical Transformations in the Environment
- **Photolysis**: sunlight-driven degradation (direct and indirect)
- **Hydrolysis**: reaction with water, pH-dependent
- **Oxidation/reduction**: redox reactions in soil/aquatic systems
- **Biodegradation**: microbial transformation (aerobic vs anaerobic)
- **Biomagnification**: concentration increases up the food chain

## Formulas
- Henry's Law: C_air = H × C_water (H in Pa·m³/mol)
- K_ow = C_octanol / C_water
- Bioaccumulation Factor: BAF = C_organism / C_environment
- Biomagnification Factor: BMF = C_predator / C_prey
