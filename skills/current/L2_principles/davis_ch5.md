# Medicines for the Future

## Source
[Medicines by Design (Davis), Chapter 5](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Biological_Chemistry/Medicines_by_Design_(Davis)/05%3A_Medicines_for_the_Future)

## Key Concepts

### Precision/Personalized Medicine
- Tailoring drug treatments to individual genetic profiles
- Pharmacogenomics: how genetic variation affects drug response
- Companion diagnostics: tests that predict drug efficacy

### Regenerative Medicine
- Stem cell therapies for tissue repair
- Growth factors and cell-signaling molecules
- Tissue engineering and organ regeneration

### Advanced Drug Modalities
- RNA therapeutics (siRNA, mRNA vaccines)
- Gene editing (CRISPR/Cas9 for therapeutic applications)
- Antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs)
- Bispecific antibodies

### Nanomedicine
- Nanoparticle drug carriers for targeted delivery
- Liposomes, polymeric nanoparticles, dendrimers
- Blood-brain barrier penetration strategies

### Careers in Pharmacology
- Academic research (colleges/universities)
- Pharmaceutical industry (drug development teams)
- Clinical pharmacology (hospitals/medical centers)
- Government agencies (NIH, FDA, EPA)
