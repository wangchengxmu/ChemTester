## Key Concepts

Drug metabolism (Phase I and Phase II) transforms compounds for elimination. Phase I: functionalization (CYP450 oxidation, reduction, hydrolysis). Phase II: conjugation (glucuronidation, sulfation, glutathione conjugation). Metabolic soft spots must be identified and addressed during lead optimization.
