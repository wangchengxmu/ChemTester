# Molecules to Medicines

## Source
[Medicines by Design (Davis), Chapter 4](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Biological_Chemistry/Medicines_by_Design_(Davis)/04%3A_Molecules_to_Medicines)

## Key Concepts

### Drug Design Challenges
- Two fundamental challenges: drug design (creating molecules with specific biological action) and drug delivery (getting the right amount to the right place)
- Balance between efficacy and side effects
- Turnaround from molecular discovery to approved medicine: typically 10-15 years, ~$1-2 billion

### Rational Drug Design
- Structure-based drug design: using 3D structures of biological targets
- Computer-aided drug design (CADD): molecular modeling, docking simulations
- Pharmacophore identification: essential molecular features for biological activity

### Drug Delivery Systems
- Oral, injectable, transdermal, inhalation, implantable routes
- Controlled-release formulations for sustained therapeutic levels
- Nanoparticle-based delivery for targeted therapy
- Prodrug strategies to improve pharmacokinetics

### High-Throughput Screening (HTS)
- Automated testing of thousands of compounds against biological targets
- Combinatorial chemistry generates large compound libraries
- Hit-to-lead optimization: improving potency, selectivity, ADME properties

### Biologics as Medicines
- Monoclonal antibodies, proteins, peptides as therapeutic agents
- Gene therapy and RNA-based medicines
- Challenges: stability, immunogenicity, delivery
