# 12 Principles of Green Chemistry — Deep Dive

## Core Concept

Green chemistry (Anastas & Warner, 1998) provides 12 design principles for chemical products and processes that minimize hazard and environmental impact. Building on entry 243, this provides deeper analysis.

---

## Detailed Principles

### 1. Prevention
"It is better to prevent waste than to treat or clean up waste after it is formed."
- **Practice:** design syntheses to minimize byproducts
- **Example:** atom-economical routes vs multi-step with protecting groups

### 2. Atom Economy (Trost, 1991)
$$\text{AE (\%)} = \frac{\text{MW(product)}}{\sum \text{MW(all reactants)}} \times 100$$
- **Limitation:** doesn't account for toxicity of waste

### 3. Less Hazardous Synthesis
- Use and generate substances of minimal toxicity
- Replace hazardous reagents (e.g., OsO₄ → catalytic MnO₄)

### 4. Designing Safer Chemicals
- Maintain function while reducing toxicity
- **Example:** replace benzene with cyclohexane; design biodegradable surfactants

### 5. Safer Solvents & Auxiliaries
- Avoid solvents; use innocuous ones when needed
- **Hierarchy:** none > water > ethanol > scCO₂ > ionic liquids

### 6. Design for Energy Efficiency
- Run reactions at ambient T and P
- Microwave, ultrasound, photochemical activation

### 7. Use of Renewable Feedstocks
- Agricultural/biological vs depleting petrochemical
- Biomass → platform molecules (HMF, levulinic acid, furfural)

### 8. Reduce Derivatives
- Minimize protecting groups, temporary modifications
- **Example:** direct amidation vs DCC coupling

### 9. Catalysis
- Catalytic (quantitative) > stoichiometric
- **Example:** biocatalysis (enzymes), organocatalysis, heterogeneous catalysis

### 10. Design for Degradation
- Products should degrade to innocuous substances
- **Example:** biodegradable polymers (PLA, PHA)

### 11. Real-time Analysis
- In-process monitoring and control
- **Technologies:** PAT (Process Analytical Technology), inline IR/NMR

### 12. Inherently Safer Chemistry
- Minimize explosion, fire, release potential
- **Example:** use aqueous H₂O₂ instead of concentrated HNO₃
