# 1.3: Properties of Hit and Lead Compounds

## Key Concepts

**Lead Sources:** Natural extracts (~35% of drugs), prior art (e.g., sildenafil→vardenafil), in silico screening (molecular docking, ΔGbind), random/targeted library screening, fragment-based screening (Rule of Three: MW<300, HBD≤3, HBA≤3, rotatable≤3, cLogP~3).

**Fragment Approaches (Rees et al. 2004):** Evolution (build out), Linking (join proximal fragments), Self-assembly (complementary reactive groups), Optimization (conventional SAR).

**Binding Measurements:** IC50 (relative, depends on assay conditions), Kd (absolute, intrinsic), Ki (via Cheng-Prusoff: Ki = IC50/(1+[S]/Km)). Hits: low-µM Kd; Leads: nM-pM Kd.

**Drug-Target Interactions:**
- Salt bridges (–20 to –40 kJ/mol, Arg/Lys/His ↔ Asp/Glu; dielectric constant: water~80, protein surface~28, interior~4)
- Ion-dipole (–12 to –20 kJ/mol, 1/r²)
- Dipole-dipole (–4 to –12 kJ/mol, 1/r³)
- H-bonding (–6 to –30 kJ/mol, 1.5-2.2 Å, optimal 180°)
- Van der Waals (–2 kJ/mol, 1/r⁶)
- π-π effects: cation-π, sandwich, edge-to-face, displaced stacking
- Halogen bonding: σ-hole (I > Br > Cl; F excluded)
- Magic methyls: entropic (water displacement) + enthalpic (hydrophobic)

**Energetics:** ΔG = ΔH – TΔS; ΔG = –RT ln Kd; residence time = 1/koff. Flexible ligands have large entropic penalty → rigidify for better Kd.
