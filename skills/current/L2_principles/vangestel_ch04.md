# Toxicology

## Source
[Environmental Toxicology (van Gestel et al.), Chapter 4](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Environmental_Chemistry/Environmental_Toxicology_(van_Gestel_et_al.)/04%3A_Toxicology)
Authors: Sylvia Moes, Kees van Gestel, & Gerco van Beek

## Key Concepts

### Principles of Toxicology
- **Dose-response relationship**: Paracelsus — "the dose makes the poison"
- Threshold vs. non-threshold effects (genotoxic carcinogens assumed non-threshold)
- Acute vs. chronic toxicity (time scale of exposure)

### Dose-Response Metrics
- **LD₅₀**: lethal dose for 50% of test population (mg/kg body weight)
- **LC₅₀**: lethal concentration (for aquatic/air exposure)
- **EC₅₀**: effective concentration for 50% effect (sublethal)
- **NOEC/LOEC**: no-observed / lowest-observed effect concentration
- **NOAEL/LOAEL**: no-observed / lowest-observed adverse effect level

### Modes of Toxic Action
- **Narcosis**: baseline toxicity (non-specific membrane disruption)
- **Specific receptor-mediated**: e.g., organophosphates inhibit acetylcholinesterase
- **Reactive/electrophilic**: covalent binding to proteins/DNA
- **Oxidative stress**: generation of reactive oxygen species (ROS)
- **Endocrine disruption**: interference with hormonal signaling

### Toxicokinetics
- ADME: Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion
- Biotransformation: Phase I (oxidation, reduction, hydrolysis) and Phase II (conjugation)
- Bioactivation: some chemicals become more toxic after metabolism (e.g., benzo[a]pyrene → BPDE)
- First-pass effect: liver metabolism before systemic circulation

### Biomarkers of Effect
- Enzyme inhibition (AChE, ALAD)
- DNA adducts, micronuclei, comet assay
- Oxidative stress markers (MDA, GSH/GSSG ratio)
- Vitellogenin induction in male fish (endocrine disruption biomarker)

## Formulas
- Hazard Quotient: HQ = Exposure / RfD (reference dose)
- Margin of Safety: MOS = NOAEL / Exposure
- Probit model for dose-response: P = Φ(a + b × log(dose))
