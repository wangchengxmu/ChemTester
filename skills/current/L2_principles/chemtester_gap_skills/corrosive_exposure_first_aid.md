# Immediate response to corrosive chemical exposure

**Retrieve with:** corrosive skin eye exposure first aid, acid alkali water flush response

**Use when:** A visible option set asks for the first response to acid, alkali, caustic, or other corrosive contact with skin or eyes.

## Procedure

1. Prioritize immediate copious water rinsing, flushing, or safety-shower use and removal of contaminated material when stated.
2. Choose the option that starts decontamination promptly, then follow the requested medical or site procedure.

- L2_principles/process_safety_analysis.md
- L2_principles/3_1_introduction_to_hazards_of_chemistry.md

## Guards

- Do not neutralize a corrosive directly on tissue because the reaction and delay can worsen injury.
- Reject covering, drying, waiting, oil or lotion, and soap-only responses when immediate flushing is available.
