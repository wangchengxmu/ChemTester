# Diastereotopic methylene protons and NMR multiplicity

**Retrieve with:** diastereotopic methylene protons, chiral center CH2 proton non-equivalence, geminal vicinal coupling ABX, pro-R pro-S replacement test

**Use when:** A proton-NMR or structure problem contains a CH2 near a stereogenic element, asks whether its two hydrogens are equivalent, or asks for their individual multiplicities.

## Procedure

1. Fix the complete structure and stereochemical state, then use the shared proton-equivalence and coupling core to apply the substitution test and NMR-timescale checks.
2. Treat nonequivalent methylene hydrogens as separate spins and list geminal, vicinal, and longer-range couplings for each proton.
3. Use a doublet-of-doublets description only for resolved first-order unequal couplings; use AB or ABX language when chemical-shift separation is comparable to J.

## Guards

- Do not call hydrogens diastereotopic merely because a chiral center is nearby; apply the substitution test to the actual structure.
- Do not combine geminal and vicinal partners into one n+1 neighbor count.
- Do not claim every diastereotopic CH2 must display two resolved peaks or a clean doublet of doublets.
- Do not confuse diastereotopic with enantiotopic substitution outcomes.
