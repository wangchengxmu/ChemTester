# Exact-scope verification of chemical application claims

**Retrieve with:** exact chemical use claim, current registered chemical uses, historical chemical applications, fumigant target commodity, sterilant versus insecticide

**Use when:** A qualitative or multi-select chemistry question asks whether a named substance is used for a stated process, target, commodity, facility, or purpose, especially when historical and current uses may differ.

## Procedure

1. Decompose each use claim into substance or formulation, function, target or commodity, venue, and time or jurisdiction.
2. Search the exact function-target pair, separating current registration or practice from historical use and prioritizing authoritative regulatory or technical sources.
3. Accept a claim only when the evidence matches its target, venue, and temporal wording; a broad use category or adjacent commodity does not entail the narrower statement.
4. Resolve apparent conflicts by distinguishing current, permitted, historical, and discontinued uses, then evaluate every option independently.
5. Return all selected option letters in the requested order and format before any explanation.

## Guards

- A generic fumigant or sterilant label does not prove a specific application.
- Historical use does not establish current use, and current nonuse does not establish that a substance was never used.
- Do not interchange similar chemical names, mixtures, or formulations.
- Empty retrieval is not supporting evidence; do not strengthen an unsupported recollection into a specific claim.
