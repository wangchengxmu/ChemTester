# Nitro-Activated Aromatic Substitution Site Triage

**Retrieve with:** nitro activated SNAr hydroxide, aryl halide to phenol, competing sulfonate hydrolysis, ipso substitution atom mapping

**Use when:** A strong nucleophile reacts with a nitro-substituted arene containing multiple plausible leaving groups or another base-sensitive linkage.

## Procedure

1. Atom-map the aromatic ring and list every bond the nucleophile could change, including aryl leaving-group bonds and hydrolysable linkages.
2. For each aryl leaving group, determine whether a nitro or other strong resonance-withdrawing group is ortho or para; prioritize those sites for classical addition-elimination SNAr.
3. Treat direct nucleophile-for-halogen replacement at an activated ipso carbon as the canonical SNAr candidate; require independent structural evidence before selecting cleavage elsewhere from reagent identity alone.
4. Construct the product by replacing only the leaving group at the selected carbon while preserving uninvolved substituents and protected functionality.
5. Back-map each candidate product and reject denitration, migration, or substitution of hydrogen unless conditions support a separate mechanism.

## Guards

- Do not apply aliphatic SN1/SN2 leaving-group heuristics to aryl halides.
- Hydroxide plus heat alone does not identify a sulfonate linkage as the selected reaction site.
- Classical SNAr is ipso substitution; preserve the original ring connectivity.
- Distinguish an O-sulfonyl protecting group from an arylsulfonyl substituent and state which bond any proposed cleavage breaks.
