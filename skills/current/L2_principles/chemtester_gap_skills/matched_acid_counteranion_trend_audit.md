# Matched acid and counteranion trend audit

**Retrieve with:** matched acid conjugate-base trend, halocarborane acid basicity, silylium carborane ion pairing, weakly coordinating anion polarizability, contact ion pair spectroscopy

**Use when:** A matched substituent series compares both Brønsted acidity and coordination or ion-pairing of the corresponding conjugate anions, especially with highly electrophilic cations and weakly coordinating cluster anions.

## Procedure

1. Write the neutral-acid and conjugate-anion series separately, and identify which property each claim ranks.
2. Rank acid strength through conjugate-base stabilization or proton affinity: a stronger acid has a less basic conjugate anion.
3. For the same cation under comparable conditions, infer direct anion coordination oppositely to acid strength, then account for substituent polarizability, softness, sterics, and charge exposure.
4. Cross-check interaction strength with association energies, cation distortion, vibrational shifts, or other direct observables before completing the statement truth set.

## Guards

- Never copy an acidity order directly onto conjugate-anion coordination without inversion and an interaction-specific check.
- Do not compare raw contact distances across different atom sizes as a standalone measure of interaction strength.
- Do not assume a weakly coordinating anion is noninteracting with a highly electrophilic cation.
- Do not transfer solid-state or solvent-specific ion-pair trends across unmatched conditions.
