# Reversible isothermal heat entropy

**Retrieve with:** reversible isothermal heat entropy q over T, entropy change heat transfer constant temperature

**Use when:** Use when a chemistry or physical-chemistry problem asks for entropy change from reversible heat transfer at a specified constant temperature.

## Procedure

1. Convert heat to joules and temperature to kelvin before calculating entropy.
2. Use Delta S = q_rev / T for the object whose entropy change is requested, preserving the sign of heat flow.
3. Report the requested units and keep any required separate final-answer line.
