# Atomic spectroscopy model-limit discrimination

**Retrieve with:** Bohr model limitations, atomic transition probabilities, energy levels versus line intensity, multielectron atomic spectra

**Use when:** A conceptual atomic-spectroscopy question asks which missing feature of a model explains an observed spectral limitation, especially in multiple-choice form.

## Procedure

1. Classify the target observable as line position or spacing, line presence or intensity, or another spectral feature.
2. Map line positions to energy-level accuracy; for multielectron atoms, test whether neglected electron–electron interaction or correlation is the stated limitation.
3. Map line presence and intensity to transition dipole matrix elements, selection rules, and transition probabilities; quantized levels alone do not supply these.
4. Compare every supplied choice verbatim, preferring the missing mechanism over its downstream consequence when the prompt asks for the reason or best explanation.

## Guards

- Never replace a choice's wording with a different nearby fact while retaining its label.
- Do not confuse energy-level prediction with transition-probability prediction.
- Invoke electron–electron interaction only when the target is multielectron energy structure and a supplied choice actually states it.
