# Michael Enolate Product Pair Matching

**Retrieve with:** Michael addition enolate product pair, beta keto ester vinyl sulfoxide cyclopentylidene ester, sodium ethoxide sodium hydride conjugate addition

**Use when:** A reaction asks for ordered major products from enolate conjugate addition or a Michael-type pair using visible structures or names.

## Procedure

1. Identify each substrate pair, the enolate donor, conjugate acceptor, base, and likely conjugate-addition site.
2. Compare visible choices by both ordered product connectivities, penalizing regioisomer, succinate, or simple substitution distractors.
