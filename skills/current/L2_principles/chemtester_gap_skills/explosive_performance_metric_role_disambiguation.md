# Explosive performance metric role disambiguation

**Retrieve with:** secondary explosive performance metrics, detonation-state characterization, Gurney velocity metal acceleration, heat of detonation thermochemistry

**Use when:** A conceptual energetic-materials task asks which quantities characterize general detonation performance or contrasts detonation-front, thermochemical, and metal-acceleration metrics.

## Procedure

1. Identify whether the requested scope is general detonation characterization, safety or sensitivity, or a specific application such as accelerating a casing or fragments.
2. Classify each candidate quantity by physical role: propagation, detonation-state pressure, thermochemical energy release, or expansion-driven metal acceleration.
3. For broad characterization, require coverage of propagation, pressure or shock intensity, and energy release; elevate a Gurney-type metric when the prompt specifically concerns casing, fragments, cylinder expansion, or delivered mechanical work.
4. Compare choices by role coverage and reject a set that replaces thermochemical energy with an application-specific acceleration surrogate under a general scope.

## Guards

- Do not treat a metric as core merely because it is widely reported.
- Keep detonation performance distinct from sensitivity, thermal stability, and handling safety unless requested.
- Preserve distinctions among heat of explosion, heat of detonation, heat of formation, and heat of combustion.
- Account for loading density, confinement, and test definition when comparing measured values.
