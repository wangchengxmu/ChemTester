# Hydroxide precipitation controlled by an ammonium buffer

**Retrieve with:** metal hydroxide Ksp ammonium buffer, precipitation pH buffer salt mass

**Use when:** A sparingly soluble metal hydroxide must precipitate at a target concentration while a weak-base conjugate-acid buffer controls hydroxide concentration.

## Procedure

1. Use the solubility product and target dissolved-metal concentration to obtain the required hydroxide concentration.
2. Convert hydroxide concentration to pH or pOH, then use the weak-base or conjugate-acid equilibrium to obtain the buffer ratio.
3. Apply the actual solution volume and molar mass to obtain the requested salt mass, preserving the requested output order.

- Respect the hydroxide stoichiometric exponent in the solubility product.
- Do not mix pre-dilution and final-volume concentration bases.
