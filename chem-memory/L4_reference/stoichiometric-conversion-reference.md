# Stoichiometric Conversion Reference

## Key equations

### Mole relationships
- `mol_B = mol_A × (coeff_B / coeff_A)`

### Mass relationships
- `mass = mol × M` (molar mass)
- `mol = mass / M`

### Particle relationships
- `particles = mol × NA` (Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10^23)
- `mol = particles / NA`

### Solution relationships
- `mol = M × V` (M = molarity, V = volume in L)
- `M = mol / V`

## Conversion factors

| From | To | Conversion |
|------|-----|------------|
| mol A | mol B | coeff ratio |
| mol | mass | × M |
| mass | mol | ÷ M |
| mol | particles | × NA |
| particles | mol | ÷ NA |
| solution | mol | M × V |

## Stoichiometry Flow Chart

```
MASS A → MOL A → MOL B → MASS B
  ↓        ↓        ↓        ↓
 ÷MA      ÷coeff  ×coeff    ×MB
          ×coeff   ÷coeff
          ratio    ratio
```

## Key concepts

### Molar mass calculation
1. Count atoms of each element in formula
2. Multiply by atomic masses
3. Sum all contributions

### Avogadro's number
- NA = 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹ (exact, by definition)
- Number of particles in exactly 12 g of C-12

### Significant figures
- Use the least precise measurement
- Molar masses typically have 4+ sig figs
- Report answers with appropriate precision
