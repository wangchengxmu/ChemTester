**Basicity order (conj. acid pKa, DMSO reference):** Pyridine (5.25) > Quinoline (4.85) > Isoquinoline (4.85) > Pyridazine (2.33) > Pyrimidine (1.30) > Pyrazine (0.60)

**General S_NAr rule:** Electron-withdrawing N activates ortho and para positions (relative to N). Two adjacent N atoms (pyrimidine C2) give maximum activation. Electron-donating substituents deactivate toward S_NAr.
