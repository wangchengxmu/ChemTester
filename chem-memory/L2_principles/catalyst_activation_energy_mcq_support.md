# Catalyst Activation Energy MCQ Support

Use this note for multiple-choice rows asking what a catalyst changes in a
reaction-rate or reversible-reaction setting.

- A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation
  energy.
- For a reversible reaction, the catalyst lowers the activation barriers for
  both forward and reverse directions and increases the rate of reaching
  equilibrium.
- A catalyst does not change the equilibrium constant, reaction enthalpy,
  Gibbs energy, or the average molecular kinetic energy at a fixed temperature.
- In MCQs, reject options that say the catalyst changes only thermodynamics or
  heats the molecules. Choose the visible option whose text says the activation
  energy is decreased for the catalyzed pathway.
