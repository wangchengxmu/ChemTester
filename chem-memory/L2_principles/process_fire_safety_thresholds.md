# Fire Safety Threshold Terms

## Flash Point

Flash point is the lowest temperature at which a liquid gives enough vapor to ignite briefly when an external ignition source is applied.

Key distinctions:
- Fire point is the lowest temperature at which vapors continue to burn after ignition; this is sustained burning and is higher than flash point.
- Autoignition temperature is the lowest temperature at which ignition occurs without an external ignition source.
- Flammability limits are concentration thresholds in air, not temperature thresholds.

## MCQ Guardrail

For definition questions, choose the option that combines:
- lowest temperature,
- brief or unsustained ignition,
- an external ignition source.

Reject options based on vapor concentration, autoignition without an ignition source, or sustained burning.
