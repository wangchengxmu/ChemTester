**Use when:** A diazonium-safety or synthesis question asks for a temperature limit, especially when preparation, operation, handling, and decomposition temperatures could be confused.

## Procedure

1. Classify every temperature by physical role: preparation setpoint, reaction temperature, generic handling or heating ceiling, or compound-specific decomposition onset.
2. For maximum or should-not-exceed wording, retrieve authoritative safety guidance using ceiling or heating terminology; do not substitute a classroom preparation temperature.
3. Match the supported temperature role to the requested role before comparing options; a smaller value is not automatically the answer to an exact-ceiling question.
4. Treat generic guidance as a screening bound only because counterion, concentration, impurities, physical state, and thermal characterization can require stricter controls.
