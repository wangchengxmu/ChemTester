# Coupled back-titration and cross-standardization balance

**Retrieve with:** coupled back titration, equivalent balance standardization, excess reagent titration, simultaneous titration equations

**Use when:** A back-titration uses one unknown reagent concentration while a separate standardization relates that concentration to another unknown titrant concentration.

## Procedure

1. Assign each concentration and volume its physical role and experiment, convert volumes consistently, and obtain equivalent factors from balanced reactions.
2. Write the separate standardization relation as νA·CA·VA = νB·CB·VB, retaining it as a relation between concentrations.
3. Write the main balance: initial reagent equivalents equal analyte equivalents consumed plus residual reagent equivalents measured by the back titration.
4. Substitute the standardization relation into the main balance, solve the requested concentration, and postpone rounding until the final output.
5. Back-substitute into both balances; require positive quantities, matching equivalent totals, correct dimensions, and the requested answer format.

## Guards

- Keep volumes from separate experiments attached to their original physical roles.
- Do not omit the analyte equivalent factor or invert the standardization volume ratio.
- A back-titrant measures residual reagent; its equivalents are not additional analyte consumption.
- Do not accept a numeric result until both independent balances reproduce it.
