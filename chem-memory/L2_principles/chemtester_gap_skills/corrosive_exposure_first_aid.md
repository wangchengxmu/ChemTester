# Immediate response to corrosive chemical exposure

**Retrieve with:** corrosive skin eye exposure first aid, acid alkali water flush response

**Use when:** An educational question asks about initial corrosive skin or eye decontamination; the substance and any product-specific instructions must be identified.

## Procedure

1. Check the substance-specific SDS and site emergency protocol; obtain trained emergency or medical assistance. For typical corrosive exposures, prompt copious water flushing is the general response.
2. Distinguish dry powder on skin, which may require careful removal before irrigation, from liquid or eye exposure; follow substance-specific exceptions without delaying indicated decontamination.

## Guards

- Do not neutralize a corrosive directly on tissue because the reaction and delay can worsen injury.
- This educational rule does not replace the SDS, trained responders, or medical care; do not invent a universal exception protocol or infer that all powders are handled alike.
