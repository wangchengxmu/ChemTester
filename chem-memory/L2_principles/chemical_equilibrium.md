# Chemical Equilibrium

## Concept Overview

Chemical equilibrium is a **dynamic state** where forward and reverse reactions occur at equal rates, resulting in constant concentrations of reactants and products.

## Key Principles

### Dynamic Nature
- Reactions continue in both directions
- Rates are equal, not stopped
- Concentrations remain constant over time

### Reversible Reactions
```
aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD
```
Double arrow (⇌) indicates reversibility.

### Rate Equality at Equilibrium
For elementary reaction A ⇌ B:
- Forward rate: rate_f = k_f[A]
- Reverse rate: rate_r = k_r[B]
- At equilibrium: k_f[A] = k_r[B]

### Law of Mass Action
At equilibrium:
```
K = k_f/k_r = [B]/[A]
```

## Problem-Solving Routes

1. **Identify equilibrium**: Check if rates are equal and concentrations constant
2. **Write equilibrium expression**: Based on balanced equation
3. **Determine K**: From rate constants or concentrations
