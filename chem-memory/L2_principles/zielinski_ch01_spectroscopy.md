# Spectroscopy — Zielinski Ch1

**Source:** Quantum States of Atoms and Molecules, Ch1 (Hanson, Zielinski, Harvey, Sweeney)

## Key Concepts

- **Spectroscopy**: study of absorption, emission, and scattering of EM radiation by atoms/molecules
- **Absorption spectrum**: I vs wavelength; three representations: transmission T, absorbance A, absorption coefficient ε
- **Beer-Lambert Law**: ε = (1/dc) log₁₀(I₀/I)

### Key Formulas

| Formula | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| T = I/I₀ | Transmission |
| A = log(I₀/I) | Absorbance (optical density) |
| ε = (1/dc) log₁₀(I₀/I) | Molar absorption coefficient |

### Types of Spectroscopy
- **Fluorescence**: fast decay (μs or faster), emission from excited states
- **Phosphorescence**: slow decay (ms to min)
- **Rayleigh scattering**: elastic (no wavelength change)
- **Raman scattering**: inelastic (wavelength change), plotted as Δwavenumber

### Spectral Band Characteristics
- **Position**: determined by energy level differences (quantum mechanics)
- **Intensity**: determined by transition moments
- **Width/shape**: due to dynamical effects (beyond book scope)

### Connection to Quantum Mechanics
- Band positions → energy level structure
- Band intensities → transition moments
- Absorption coefficient ε calculable from transition moment (quantum mechanical quantity)
