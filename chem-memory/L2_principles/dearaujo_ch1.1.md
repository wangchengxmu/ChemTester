## Key Concepts

Medicinal chemistry traces to Paul Ehrlich's "magic bullet" concept for syphilis. Modern drug discovery is highly integrative: basic research → target selection → hit-to-lead → lead optimization → preclinical/clinical development → regulatory approval. True medicinal chemists are versed in all facets: chemical synthesis, pharmacology, molecular biology, bioanalytical chemistry. The classical pipeline is linear/iterative, with simultaneous evaluation of PK properties for oral bioavailability.
