## Key Concepts

Drug absorption and distribution are governed by the physicochemical properties of the drug and the biological barriers it must cross. Key concepts include membrane permeability (passive diffusion, active transport), bioavailability, volume of distribution, and the roles of lipophilicity (LogP), molecular weight, and ionization state.
