# Gas Law Manometer Unit Conversion Support

Use this note when a gas-law problem gives a gas sample temperature and a water manometer reading.

- In PV = nRT, use the gas sample temperature. A separate manometer liquid temperature is normally used only to choose the liquid density for pressure conversion.
- Convert a water-column height to pressure with P = rho g h. Use SI units: rho in kg m^-3, g in m s^-2, and h in m.
- Convert dm3 to m3 before using SI pressure, and convert gas mass to moles using the molar mass.
- If the prompt reports the manometer height as the sample pressure, do not add atmospheric pressure. If it reports a gauge pressure relative to atmosphere, follow the stated convention.
- A common error is to use the water-density temperature as the gas temperature. Keep those roles separate.
