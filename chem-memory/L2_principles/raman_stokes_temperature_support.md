# Raman Stokes Anti-Stokes Ground-State Temperature Support

Use this note for Raman spectroscopy multiple-choice questions involving wavelength shifts, energy direction, and temperature.

- Retrieval anchors: Raman, spectroscopy, Stokes, anti-Stokes, scattered light, longer wavelength, shorter wavelength, ground-state population, vibrational energy, polarizable electron cloud, statements I II III IV V.
- Raman scattered light with longer wavelength than the incident light has lower photon energy. The molecule gains the energy difference as vibrational excitation; this is a Stokes line.
- Raman scattered light with shorter wavelength than the incident light has higher photon energy. The molecule loses vibrational energy; this is an anti-Stokes line.
- Lower temperature increases the relative population of the vibrational ground state and decreases the population of excited vibrational states.
- In questions that say "based on these observations", separate statements implied by the observations from generally true Raman facts. Do not include a true but unobserved property unless the prompt asks for all generally true Raman statements.
- A statement that Stokes scattering transfers energy to the molecule is compatible with longer-wavelength scattered light. A statement that the scattered photon has higher energy is not.
