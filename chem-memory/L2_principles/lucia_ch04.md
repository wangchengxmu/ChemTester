# Alternative Solvents

## Source
[Key Elements of Green Chemistry (Lucia), Chapter 4](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Environmental_Chemistry/Key_Elements_of_Green_Chemistry_(Lucia)/04%3A_Alternative_Solvents)
Author: Lucian Lucia, North Carolina State University

## Key Concepts

### The Solvent Problem
- Solvents are the matrix/medium for most chemical reactions and processes
- Traditional volatile organic solvents (VOCs): benzene, chloroform, hexane — toxic, flammable
- Solvents often constitute 80-90% of mass in pharmaceutical manufacturing waste
- VOC emissions contribute to air pollution, ozone formation, health hazards

### Green Solvent Categories
1. **Water**: universal, non-toxic, cheap; limitations: poor organic solubility
2. **Supercritical CO₂ (scCO₂)**: tunable solvent properties, non-toxic, easily removed
3. **Ionic liquids (ILs)**: negligible vapor pressure, tunable properties; concerns: persistence, toxicity
4. **Deep eutectic solvents (DES)**: biodegradable, cheap, from natural compounds
5. **Fluorinated solvents**: perfluorinated compounds; persistence concerns
6. **Bio-based solvents**: ethanol, 2-methyl-THF, cyrene, γ-valerolactone
7. **Switchable solvents**: properties change with CO₂ addition/removal

### Solvent Selection Frameworks
- **CHEM21 solvent selection guide**: GSK-developed, classifies solvents as recommended/ problematic/undesirable
- **Hansen solubility parameters**: predict solvent-solute compatibility
- **COSMO-RS**: computational solvent screening
- Life cycle assessment of solvent production and disposal

### Solvent-Free and Neat Reactions
- Mechanically activated (ball milling, grinding)
- Microwave-assisted solid-phase reactions
- On-water catalysis (reactions at water surface)

## Formulas
- Hansen solubility parameter: δ_total² = δ_d² + δ_p² + δ_h²
- ScCO₂ critical point: T_c = 31.1°C, P_c = 7.38 MPa
