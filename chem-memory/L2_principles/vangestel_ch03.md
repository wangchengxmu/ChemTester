# Environmental Chemistry — From Fate to Exposure

## Source
[Environmental Toxicology (van Gestel et al.), Chapter 3](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Environmental_Chemistry/Environmental_Toxicology_(van_Gestel_et_al.)/03%3A_Environmental_Chemistry_-_From_Fate_to_Exposure)
Authors: Sylvia Moes, Kees van Gestel, & Gerco van Beek

## Key Concepts

### Environmental Fate
- Processes determining where chemicals go and how long they persist
- Transport: advection (bulk flow), diffusion, dispersion
- Partitioning between compartments (air-water-soil-biota)
- Multimedia fugacity models for chemical distribution

### Degradation Pathways
- **Abiotic**: photodegradation, hydrolysis, redox reactions
- **Biotic**: microbial metabolism (aerobic/anaerobic)
- Half-life (t₁/₂): time for 50% degradation
- Persistence: chemicals with t₁/₂ > 2 months in water, > 6 months in soil are "persistent"

### Exposure Assessment
- Exposure routes: inhalation, ingestion, dermal contact
- Exposure pathway analysis: source → transport → exposure point → intake
- Bioavailability: fraction of total chemical that is biologically available
- Biomonitoring: measuring chemicals or metabolites in biological samples

### Bioaccumulation and Biomagnification
- **Bioconcentration**: uptake from water only (aquatic organisms)
- **Bioaccumulation**: from all routes (water, food, sediment)
- **Biomagnification**: increasing concentration across trophic levels
- K_ow threshold: log K_ow > 5 suggests significant bioaccumulation potential

### Environmental Monitoring
- Passive sampling: semipermeable membrane devices (SPMDs)
- Biomonitoring organisms: mussels, lichens, indicator species
- Environmental quality standards (EQS) for water/sediment/soil

## Formulas
- First-order degradation: C_t = C₀ × e^(-kt)
- Half-life: t₁/₂ = ln(2) / k
- BCF = C_fish / C_water (bioconcentration factor)
- Daily Intake: DI = C_medium × IR (concentration × intake rate)
