# Natural Product Classes

## Core Concept

Natural products are organic compounds produced by living organisms. They are classified by structural features or biosynthetic origin into several major classes, each with characteristic structures and biological activities.

---

## Major Classes

### 1. Terpenes / Isoprenoids
- **Building block:** isoprene (C₅) — head-to-tail coupling
- **Classification by number of isoprene units:**

| Class | Units | Carbons | Examples |
|-------|-------|---------|----------|
| Monoterpene | 2 | C₁₀ | Limonene, menthol, pinene |
| Sesquiterpene | 3 | C₁₅ | Farnesene, artemisinin |
| Diterpene | 4 | C₂₀ | Taxol, gibberellic acid |
| Triterpene | 6 | C₃₀ | Squalene, lanosterol |
| Tetraterpene | 8 | C₄₀ | β-carotene, lycopene |
| Polyterpene | >8 | C₅ₙ | Natural rubber (cis-1,4-polyisoprene) |

### 2. Alkaloids
- Nitrogen-containing basic compounds (usually from amino acids)
- **Examples:**
  - Morphine (opium poppy) — analgesic
  - Quinine (Cinchona) — antimalarial
  - Caffeine (coffee, tea) — stimulant
  - Nicotine (tobacco) — stimulant/toxic
  - Cocaine (coca) — local anesthetic
  - Atropine (belladonna) — anticholinergic
  - Strychnine (Strychnos) — pesticide

### 3. Steroids
- **Skeleton:** cyclopentanophenanthrene (fused 4-ring system)
- **Cholesterol:** precursor to all steroids
- **Classes:**
  - Sex hormones: testosterone, estradiol, progesterone
  - Corticosteroids: cortisol, aldosterone
  - Vitamin D: calciferol
  - Bile acids: cholic acid
  - Cardiac glycosides: digoxin

### 4. Polyketides
- **Building block:** acetate (C₂) or propionate (C₃) units
- **Assembly:** polyketide synthase (PKS) — similar to fatty acid synthase
- **Examples:**
  - Erythromycin (macrolide antibiotic)
  - Tetracycline (antibiotic)
  - Doxorubicin (anticancer)
  - Amphotericin B (antifungal)
  - Aflatoxin (mycotoxin)

### 5. Phenylpropanoids
- Derived from phenylalanine or tyrosine
- **Examples:** flavonoids, lignin, coumarins, stilbenes (resveratrol)

### 6. Fatty Acids & Lipids
- Linear carboxylic acids (C₄-C₂₈)
- Saturated, monounsaturated, polyunsaturated
- Prostaglandins, leukotrienes (eicosanoids, C₂₀)

## Complete Extraction: Natural Product Classification (Roberts & Caserio Ch30)

### Definition and Scope
Natural products are organic compounds produced by living organisms. They include:
- **Primary metabolites:** Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, nucleic acids (direct metabolic roles)
- **Secondary metabolites:** Alkaloids, terpenes, mold metabolites (no obvious metabolic function; may be "metabolic accidents" or defense compounds)

### Classification Approaches
1. **By recurring structural feature** (most common in organic chemistry)
2. **By plant genus/source**
3. **By biosynthetic origin** (modern approach)

### Key Classes from Roberts & Caserio Ch30

#### Isoprenoid Compounds (Terpenes)
- Derived from isoprene units (C₅)
- **Monoterpenes (C₁₀):** Menthol, limonene, pinene (essential oils from steam distillation)
- **Sesquiterpenes (C₁₅):** Farnesol, patchoulol
- **Diterpenes (C₂₀):** Taxol (anticancer), vitamin A
- **Triterpenes (C₃₀):** Squalene, lanosterol (steroid precursors)
- **Tetraterpenes (C₄₀):** β-carotene
- **Polyterpenes:** Natural rubber

#### Steroids
- Contain **hydrogenated cyclopentanophenanthrene** skeleton
- Most are alcohols (sterols): cholesterol, ergosterol, estradiol, stigmasterol
- Cholesterol: precursor to bile acids, steroid hormones, vitamin D

#### Alkaloids (Nitrogen-Containing Natural Products)
- Basic nitrogen compounds from plants
- Remarkably heterogeneous class with complex structures
- Examples: morphine, caffeine, nicotine, quinine, strychnine
- Many are pharmacologically active (drugs, poisons)

#### Prostaglandins
- 20-carbon lipid compounds with hormone-like effects
- Derived enzymatically from fatty acids
- Contain a 5-carbon ring
- Subclass of eicosanoids and prostanoids
- Found in nearly every human tissue

### Value of Natural Products
- **Drugs:** Morphine (analgesic), taxol (anticancer), quinine (antimalarial)
- **Flavorings/Fragrances:** Menthol, vanillin, limonene
- **Dyes:** Indigo, alizarin
- **Poisons:** Strychnine, curare
- **Agriculture:** Pyrethrins (insecticides)

### [Source: Wikipedia, Polyketide]
### Polyketide Overview
Polyketides are secondary metabolites assembled from acetate (C鈧? and propionate (C鈧? units by polyketide synthases (PKSs).

| Type | PKS Module | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Type I (modular) | Domain-based, each module adds one unit | Erythromycin, Rapamycin |
| Type II (iterative) | Single set of domains used repeatedly | Tetracycline, Doxorubicin |
| Type III (chalcone synthase-like) | Minimal PKS | Flavonoids, Stilbenes |
