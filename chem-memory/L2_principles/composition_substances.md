## Formula Mass / Molar Mass

Formula mass (amu) = sum of atomic masses of all atoms in formula unit.
Molar mass (g/mol) = numerically same as formula mass.

Example: H₂O = 2(1.008) + 15.999 = 18.015 g/mol.

## Percent Composition

% element = (n × atomic mass of element / molar mass of compound) × 100

Where n = number of atoms of that element in the formula.

## Determining Empirical Formulas

Steps:
1. Assume 100 g sample → mass % = grams of each element
2. Convert each mass to moles (divide by atomic mass)
3. Find simplest whole-number mole ratio
4. If ratios are not whole numbers, multiply all by smallest integer to clear fractions

## Molecular Formula from Empirical Formula

Molecular formula = (empirical formula)ₙ
where n = molar mass / empirical formula mass

## Molarity

M = n / V = moles of solute / liters of solution

Units: mol/L (also written M). Note: volume is of the **solution**, not just solvent.

## Solution Preparation

Mass to make a solution of desired molarity:
mass (g) = M × V(L) × molar_mass (g/mol)

## Dilution

M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

Where M₁, V₁ are initial concentration and volume; M₂, V₂ are final.
This holds because moles of solute are conserved during dilution.
