# Body, Heal Thyself

## Source
[Medicines by Design (Davis), Chapter 2](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Biological_Chemistry/Medicines_by_Design_(Davis)/02%3A_Body_Heal_Thyself)

## Key Concepts

### The Body as a Chemical Machine
- 15th-16th century scientific revolution: body systems described (circulatory, digestive, respiratory, nervous, excretory)
- Body converts food to energy via chemical reactions (metabolism)

### Enzymes as Biological Catalysts
- Enzymes speed up biochemical reactions without being consumed
- Each enzyme has a specific substrate (lock-and-key model)
- Enzyme deficiencies cause metabolic diseases
- Many drugs work by inhibiting specific enzymes

### Hormones as Chemical Messengers
- Endocrine system: hormones regulate growth, metabolism, reproduction, mood
- Insulin regulates blood glucose levels (diabetes when deficient)
- Hormone receptors are drug targets (e.g., beta-blockers for cardiovascular disease)
- Steroid hormones (cortisol, estrogen, testosterone) act via nuclear receptors

### The Immune System
- Innate and adaptive immunity
- Antibodies: Y-shaped proteins that recognize specific antigens
- Monoclonal antibody drugs: engineered versions for targeted therapy
- Autoimmune diseases: immune system attacks self (rheumatoid arthritis, lupus)

### Receptors and Signal Transduction
- G-protein coupled receptors (GPCRs): largest family of drug targets (~35% of approved drugs)
- Ion channels: voltage-gated and ligand-gated
- Kinase inhibitors: target signaling cascades in cancer

## Formulas
- Michaelis-Menten enzyme kinetics: v = V_max [S] / (K_m + [S])
- K_m = substrate concentration at half-maximal velocity
