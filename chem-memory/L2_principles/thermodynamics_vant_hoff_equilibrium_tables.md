# Van't Hoff Equilibrium Constants Decomposition Enthalpy Support

Use this note when a problem gives equilibrium constants at several temperatures and asks for a reaction enthalpy.

- Formula-query anchors: equilibrium constants, reaction enthalpy, decomposition, metal carbonate, Ag2CO3, Ag2O, CO2, mol.
- For a reaction with approximately temperature-independent standard enthalpy, use the integrated van't Hoff relation: ln K = -DeltaH°/(R T) + DeltaS°/R.
- A plot or linear fit of ln K versus 1/T has slope -DeltaH°/R. Therefore DeltaH° = -R times slope.
- Use kelvin for T and R = 8.314 J mol^-1 K^-1. Convert J mol^-1 to kJ mol^-1 at the end.
- For decompositions that produce a gas from solids, the equilibrium constant may increase strongly with temperature; that corresponds to a positive decomposition enthalpy.
- Do not fit K directly against T. Transform to ln K and 1/T first, and preserve the sign from the slope.
