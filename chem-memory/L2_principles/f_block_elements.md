# f-Block Elements - Lanthanides and Actinides

## Concept Overview

The f-block elements comprise the lanthanides (4f series) and actinides (5f series), also known as inner transition metals. They exhibit unique properties due to the filling of f orbitals.

**Source:** LibreTexts Inorganic Chemistry - Housecroft Chapter 27

---

## Key Principles

### Definition and Location
- **Lanthanides:** Elements 57-71 (La to Lu), filling 4f orbitals
- **Actinides:** Elements 89-103 (Ac to Lr), filling 5f orbitals
- Located below the main body of the periodic table
- Maximum 14 electrons can occupy f orbitals (n-2 rule)

### General Properties
| Property | Lanthanides | Actinides |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Radioactivity | Some (Pm) | All radioactive |
| Oxidation states | Mostly +3 | +3 to +6 common |
| Orbital filling | 4f before 5d | 5f competes with 6d |
| Shielding | 4f poor shielding | 5f poor shielding |
| Reactivity | Electropositive | Highly reactive |
| Magnetic | Many paramagnetic | Many paramagnetic |

---

## Electronic Structure

### f-Orbital Characteristics
- **7 orbitals** (ml = -3 to +3)
- **Complex shapes** with lobes pointing in many directions
- **Poor shielding** - f electrons don't protect outer electrons from nuclear charge
- **Energy ordering:** After La, 4f drops below 5d

### Electron Configurations

#### Lanthanides (4f series)
```
La: [Xe] 5d¹ 6s²          (5d fills first)
Ce: [Xe] 4f¹ 5d¹ 6s²      (4f begins filling)
Pr: [Xe] 4f³ 6s²          (irregular, skips 5d)
...
Eu: [Xe] 4f⁷ 6s²          (half-filled f)
Gd: [Xe] 4f⁷ 5d¹ 6s²      (half-filled f, adds d)
...
Lu: [Xe] 4f¹⁴ 5d¹ 6s²     (filled f)
```

#### Actinides (5f series)
```
Ac: [Rn] 6d¹ 7s²
Th: [Rn] 6d² 7s²
Pa: [Rn] 5f² 6d¹ 7s²
U:  [Rn] 5f³ 6d¹ 7s²
Np: [Rn] 5f⁴ 6d¹ 7s²
Pu: [Rn] 5f⁶ 7s²          (irregular)
...
Lr: [Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d¹ 7s²
```

### Key Differences: 4f vs 5f
| Aspect | 4f (Lanthanides) | 5f (Actinides) |
|--------|------------------|----------------|
| Radial extension | More contracted | More extended |
| Overlap with ligands | Minimal | Significant |
| Bonding character | Mostly ionic | More covalent possible |
| Oxidation states | Limited (+3 dominant) | Wider range |
| Shielding effectiveness | Very poor | Poor |

## Oxidation States

### Lanthanide Oxidation States
**Dominant:** +3 for all lanthanides

**Exceptions:**
- Ce⁴⁺ (stable, used in redox chemistry)
- Eu²⁺ (stable due to f⁷ configuration)
- Yb²⁺ (stable due to f¹⁴ configuration)
- Tb⁴⁺ (less common)

### Actinide Oxidation States
**Wide range possible:**
| Element | Common Oxidation States | Most Stable |
|---------|------------------------|-------------|
| Ac | +3 | +3 |
| Th | +3, +4 | +4 |
| Pa | +3, +4, +5 | +4, +5 |
| U | +3, +4, +5, +6 | +4, +6 |
| Np | +3 to +7 | +4, +5, +6 |
| Pu | +3 to +7 | +3, +4 |
| Am | +3 to +7 | +3 |
| Later actinides | +2, +3 | +3 |

## Coordination Chemistry

### Coordination Numbers
- **Lanthanides:** 8-12 common (high due to large ions)
- **Actinides:** 6-12 common
- **Trend:** Coordination number decreases across series

### Ligand Preferences
- **Hard Lewis acids** - prefer O and F donors
- **Lanthanides:** Strongly prefer oxygen donors
- **Actinides:** Can form more covalent bonds with softer donors

### Typical Complexes
- **Aqua ions:** [Ln(H₂O)₈-9]³⁺
- **EDTA complexes:** Very stable
- **β-diketonates:** Common in extraction chemistry
- **Cyclopentadienyl:** Organometallic compounds (especially U, Th)

---

## Magnetic Properties

### Lanthanide Magnetism
- Many lanthanide ions are **paramagnetic** (unpaired f electrons)
- **Diamagnetic ions:** La³⁺ (f⁰), Lu³⁺ (f¹⁴), Yb²⁺, Ce⁴⁺
- **Spin-orbit coupling** is very strong (heavy elements)
- **Orbital contribution** not quenched (unlike d-block)
- Effective magnetic moments calculated from total angular momentum J

### Actinide Magnetism
- More complex due to 5f orbital involvement in bonding
- Spin-orbit coupling even stronger than lanthanides
- Magnetic behavior varies with oxidation state

## Color and Spectroscopy

### Lanthanide Colors (from f-f transitions)
| Ion | Unpaired e⁻ | Color |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| La³⁺ | 0 | Colorless |
| Ce³⁺ | 1 | Colorless |
| Pr³⁺ | 2 | Green |
| Nd³⁺ | 3 | Reddish |
| Eu³⁺ | 6 | Pale pink |
| Gd³⁺ | 7 | Colorless |
| Tb³⁺ | 6 | Pale pink |
| Dy³⁺ | 5 | Yellow |
| Er³⁺ | 3 | Reddish |
| Tm³⁺ | 2 | Green |

### f-f Transitions
- **Laporte-forbidden** (Δl = 0)
- **Very sharp bands** (shielded from ligand field)
- **Weak intensity** (ε ~ 1-10 M⁻¹cm⁻¹)
- **Ligand field effects** minimal (unlike d-block)

## Occurrence and Extraction

### Lanthanide Sources
- **Monazite:** Light lanthanides (La-Nd)
- **Xenotime:** Heavy lanthanides (Tb-Lu)
- **Bastnäsite:** Light lanthanides

### Actinide Sources
- **Pitchblende (U₃O₈):** Uranium ore
- **Monazite:** Contains thorium
- Transuranium elements: Synthetic

### Separation Methods
1. **Solvent extraction** (organophosphorus extractants)
2. **Ion exchange chromatography**
3. **Selective oxidation/reduction**
4. **Fractional crystallization** (historical)

---

## Applications

### Lanthanides
- **Catalysts:** Petroleum refining, automotive catalysts
- **Magnets:** Nd-Fe-B permanent magnets
- **Phosphors:** LED lights, displays (Eu, Tb)
- **Glass polishing:** CeO₂
- **Lasers:** Nd:YAG lasers

### Actinides
- **Nuclear fuel:** U, Pu in reactors
- **Weapons:** U-235, Pu-239
- **Smoke detectors:** Am-241
- **RTG power sources:** Pu-238 for space missions
- **Medicine:** Radiotherapy isotopes
