# Inorganic Gas Trap Stoichiometry

## Applicability

Gas-analysis or inorganic decomposition tasks pass products through drying, carbon dioxide absorption, red-hot copper, or STP volume and pressure measurements.

## Procedure

1. Identify which trap removes water, which removes carbon dioxide, and whether red-hot copper consumes an oxidizing gas while leaving nitrogen.
2. Balance source decomposition equations and any copper oxidation equation before converting trap mass gains to moles.
3. Use residual gas volume, pressure, and temperature on a consistent basis to close the mole balance.
4. Report the requested mole ratio, composition, or visible choice only after checking all trapped and untrapped gas streams.

## Boundaries

- Do not treat every mass increase as water; match each trap to the species it retains or reacts with.
- Check whether an oxide-forming gas is consumed before assigning the residual gas identity.
