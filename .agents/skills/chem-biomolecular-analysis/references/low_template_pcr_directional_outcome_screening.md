# Low-template PCR directional outcome screening

## Applicability

Use for qualitative PCR or amplification questions that vary starting-template amount and ask for an expected or least-expected outcome.

## Procedure

1. Parse polarity words such as least, not, and except; restate whether the target is a consistent or inconsistent consequence.
2. At fixed cycling and reagent conditions, lowering initial template lowers expected product or signal and increases molecule-sampling noise.
3. Classify dropout, allele or peak imbalance, and weak or failed amplification as low-input-consistent effects; classify increased yield or signal as inconsistent unless compensation is stated.
4. For a least-expected prompt choose the directionally inconsistent outcome, then recheck the prompt polarity before answering.

## Boundaries

- Do not select a genuine adverse low-template artifact merely because it is not universal; test causal direction first.
- Do not assume compensatory changes such as additional cycles unless stated.
- Keep total yield or signal distinct from profile balance and completeness.
