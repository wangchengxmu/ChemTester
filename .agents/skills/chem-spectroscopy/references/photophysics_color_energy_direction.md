# Photophysics Color Energy Direction

## Applicability

A question asks for emitted, observed, or absorbed visible color from photon energy, wavelength, or conjugated-dye wording.

## Procedure

1. Convert photon energy to wavelength, or use the given emitted or observed color only after identifying the requested direction.
2. For nonfluorescent transmitted or reflected color under broad illumination, a complementary absorption band is only an approximate color relation. Fluorescence emission alone does not determine the absorption band; require an absorption spectrum, transition data, or an explicitly stated idealized convention.
3. Check wavelength labels against the specified optical process. If the prompt explicitly imposes a complementary-color convention, identify it as an assumption rather than a general law of fluorescence.

## Boundaries

- Keep emitted, observed, transmitted, and absorbed color directions distinct.
- Do not infer absorption wavelength from emission wavelength alone, or ignore inconsistent numeric labels. Stokes shifts and spectral bandwidths require additional information.
- Use only available documented support; otherwise apply the explicit derivation or label missing empirical evidence unresolved. A library filename is not an executable tool.
