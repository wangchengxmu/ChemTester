# Fixed-volume real-gas enthalpy change

## Applicability

A closed real gas at fixed amount and volume receives heat or changes temperature and the requested quantity is enthalpy rather than internal energy.

## Procedure

1. Use the fixed-volume heat as the internal-energy change only when the stated process justifies it.
2. Compute enthalpy from delta H equals delta U plus delta of PV, evaluating each pressure from the stated equation of state.
3. Apply pressure-volume unit conversion once and report the final enthalpy with its sign and requested units.

## Boundaries

- Do not stop at heat input when the question asks for enthalpy.
- Do not discard covolume or attraction terms without checking their cancellation under the stated constraints.
