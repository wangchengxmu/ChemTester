# Michael Enolate Product Pair Matching

## Applicability

A reaction asks for ordered major products from enolate conjugate addition or a Michael-type pair using visible structures or names.

## Procedure

1. Identify each substrate pair, the enolate donor, conjugate acceptor, base, and likely conjugate-addition site.
2. Map the donor carbon to the acceptor beta carbon under the stated conditions, track protonation and workup, then compare both ordered product connectivities without penalizing any product name in advance.

## Boundaries

- Do not reuse a letter mapping when the choices are relettered or reordered.
